About us

Welcome to the Intelligent Unmanned Systems Laboratory (IUSL) at University of Macau! Our research bridges the gap between theory and practice by focusing on Artificial Intelligence, Dynamic Systems and Control, and Optimization, applied to Robotic and Autonomous Systems. We aim to drive the evolution of next-generation autonomous unmanned systems, spanning unmanned aerial vehicles, autonomous vehicles, and quadrupedal robots.
